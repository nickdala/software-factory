# Intent: Related posts under each post

> Request: Show a few related posts at the end of each post

## Problem
A reader who finishes a post has nowhere obvious to go next. They leave.

## Who it is for
Readers who reach the end of a post and want more on the same topic.

## Desired outcome
The end of each post suggests a few other posts on the same topic.

## How we will know it worked
Every post with tagged siblings ends with a short list of them, and posts
with no siblings show nothing extra.

## Constraints
- Use the tags posts already have. No new data or services.
- Keep the post layout as it is above the list.

## Open questions
1. **List length**: should the list show 3 posts?
   Recommended: 3, enough to offer a choice without crowding the end of the post.
